# Submitting news and stories

This guide is the first version of the Dura Students Society's editor-mediated
process for news, announcements, event reports, and community stories.

## How publishing works

1. **Send a draft to a Society editor.** Use the submission template below and
   send it through a communication channel already used by the Society. The
   website does not currently have a submission form or a confirmed editorial
   email address.
2. **The editor acknowledges and reviews it.** The editor may ask questions,
   request a shorter version, or suggest that a longer story be published
   elsewhere and linked from the News page.
3. **The editor confirms permissions and details.** The submitter should verify
   names, dates, spellings, and factual claims, and confirm that the people
   quoted or shown in submitted media have agreed to its use. The editor checks
   that the material is appropriate to share publicly and follows the Society's
   rights and consent guidance.
4. **A site maintainer prepares the post.** News is currently maintained in
   `data/news.ts`. The maintainer adds the approved post and opens a pull
   request; automated checks run before it is merged and deployed.
5. **The editor checks the published post.** The editor verifies the text,
   date, links, and any media on the live News page. Report corrections to the
   editor so the source can be updated.

Submitting a draft does not guarantee publication or a particular publication
date. The editor will tell the submitter if the Society cannot publish it or if
more information is needed.

## Submission template

Copy and complete the fields that apply. Write `Not applicable` where a field
does not apply.

```text
Proposed title:
Category: Announcement / Event report / Community story / Language & heritage
Proposed publication date (if time-sensitive):
Event or story date (if different):
Submitted by (name and preferred contact):
Suggested byline (optional):

Short summary (1–2 sentences):
Draft text:

Names, dates, places, and spellings to verify:
Sources or people who can confirm the details:

Related link (optional):
Suggested link text (optional):

Photo or other media (optional; describe or share separately):
Caption and credit:
Who created or owns this media?
Have the people shown or recorded agreed to this public use?

Is any part private, sensitive, or not intended for public sharing?
Anything else the editor should know:
```

Do not send original, irreplaceable media or private records unless the editor
asks for them and agrees on a suitable way to transfer them. Do not include
personal contact details, private information, or community knowledge that is
not intended for public sharing in the proposed article.

## What the site can publish today

The News page supports a category, a display date, short paragraphs, an
optional related link, and links to background sources. A longer story can be
represented by a short summary and a link to an approved full version. By-lines
and photographs are not yet part of the News post format; the editor and
maintainer will agree how to handle these before publication rather than
promising a feature the site does not currently provide.

Dates may be supplied in Bikram Sambat or Gregorian format for display. The
maintainer also needs the corresponding Gregorian date in `YYYY-MM-DD` format
to sort posts correctly; the editor should confirm this conversion for the
submitter when needed.

News posts based on information published on the Society's website should link
to those sources, as the current posts do. Confirm any additional event details,
names, dates, or personal accounts with the Society before including them.
